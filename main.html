About关于我

我是 Tufts 大二学生，主修认知与脑科学。

大二之前，我学的是视觉传达设计。从设计到认知科学，我一直好奇的是同一件事：我们怎么看见、认识、理解这个世界并与之互动。

现在，这个问题从人延伸到了机器：机器如何感知世界，又如何丰富人们探索世界的方式。

寻找 connection，创造 connection，不停探索。

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Cognitive & Brain Sciences at Tufts University. Before my sophomore year, I studied communication design. From design to cognitive science, I've been curious about the same thing: how we see, come to know, understand, and interact with this world. That question now extends from people to machines: how machines perceive the world, and how they can enrich the way people explore it.

Seek connection, create connection, keep exploring.

Research研究

Tufts AI Design Accessibility (TADA) Lab

Principal Investigator: Prof. Jaylin Herskovitz 导师 Prof. Jaylin Herskovitz

Research Assistant · Jun 2026 – present 研究助理 · 2026.6 至今

负责 A11yExtensions 新手引导流程的设计与实现。该项目是一个 iOS 原型，用快捷指令把已发表的盲人无障碍研究成果带进盲人日常使用的 app。从文字脚本、手绘分镜、Figma 可交互原型一路做到 SwiftUI 实现，目前完成七个页面，三个扩展共用同一套界面，新增扩展只需添加一条数据。真机测试发现安装与授权环节存在多处静默失败，包括 VoiceOver 在权限弹窗上不移动焦点，以及 app 交接给快捷指令后完全失声。据此重新设计了授权路径，并将一次强制测试加入引导流程。

I design and build the onboarding flow for A11yExtensions, an iOS prototype that uses Shortcuts to bring published accessibility research for blind users into the apps they already use every day. From text scripts, hand-drawn storyboards, and Figma interactive prototypes to SwiftUI implementation, currently seven pages have been completed. Three extensions share the same interface. Testing on a real device revealed several silent failures during setup and permissions, including VoiceOver focus not moving to the permission alert and speech cutting out completely when the app hands off to Shortcuts. Based on this, I redesigned the authorization path, and a mandatory test was added to the boot process

IDEA Lab, Tufts University

Design Evolution project · Fall 2026 Design Evolution 项目 · 2026 秋

参与 Design Evolution 前端开发。该项目是一个网页工具，帮助人们设计出说不清、画不出的东西：用户在一组方案里反复挑出最接近心中所想的，算法据此生成下一轮，设计一轮轮“进化”。我在 Build Team 用 React 开发前端，把设计组和用户研究组提出的界面改进落地为可用功能，并参与把探索器接入 3D 生成模型。

I work on the front-end development of Design Evolution, which is a web tool that helps users come up with designs that are difficult to describe or draw. Users repeatedly select the option that is closest to the mental representation of their need. The algorithm generates the next round based on their choices, and the design thus "evolves" round by round. On the Build Team, I use React to turn interface improvements from the design and user research teams into working features, and I'm helping connect the explorer to 3D generative models.
